# Credits

## Original artwork

Buildings, icons, cards, dice, textures, effects, level banners and the top-down hero sprites
are drawn by the scripts in `Tools/art/draw.py` and `Tools/art/cards.py`: original work of this
project. Text is rendered with Aileron (CC0), bundled with Pillow.

The intro story images (`Assets/Sprites/sceneimages/startimage2-6.png`) were generated by the
game's author with Microsoft Image Creator from Designer (the files carry its C2PA signature,
dated 2024-10-30).

## Artwork: characters and monsters (LPC)

All monsters, the hero portraits and the captain are composed from layers of the
Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
(https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator),
combined, recoloured and cropped by the scripts in `Tools/art`. Every layer is used under an
attribution-only licence (OGA-BY / CC-BY) or CC0; share-alike and GPL-only layers are not used.
