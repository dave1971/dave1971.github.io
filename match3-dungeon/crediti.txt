(https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator),
recoloured and cropped to the front-facing rows. Each layer is used under the licence shown
(attribution only). Modified: layers combined, recoloured, some made semi-transparent.
